# Product Guidelines: Federated Brain System

## Prose Style

The prose style for the Federated Brain System will be **Informative and Technical**, focusing on clear, concise, and accurate descriptions of technical details and functionalities. This approach ensures that documentation and communication are precise and unambiguous, catering to an audience that requires detailed understanding of the system's inner workings.

Additionally, the prose will be **Formal and Professional**, maintaining a serious and authoritative tone. This includes adhering to strict grammatical rules, avoiding colloquialisms, and ensuring that all communication reflects a high standard of professionalism suitable for an advanced operating system.
